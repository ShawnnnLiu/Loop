Parent FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is this just a chatbot?

No — it's a system that plans, schedules, and tracks real progress. The chat is the smallest part.

Is it spying on my kid?

No. You see summary progress only. Essays and private notes are never shared.

Will it replace our school counselor or consultant?

No. It runs the repetitive accountability work; humans stay for judgment.

How does billing work?

Cadence ($39/mo) is cancel-anytime — stop whenever you like; a month already charged isn't refunded, and you keep access through that month. Stride (~$33/mo, billed as $398 once for the season) and Lodestar ($2,688) are one-time upfront charges and are non-refundable — so we keep the entry price low and let you try Cadence first.
