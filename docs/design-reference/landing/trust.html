Seven promises

Seven promises — and why each matters.

1

Nothing touches your calendar without your approval.

You're always the last word.

2

No double-bookings or messes — ever, even if something crashes.

Your real calendar stays clean.

3

Your private writing stays private; parents see only what you permit.

Trust between you and your family, protected by design.

4

The system always tells you why.

Every change comes with a plain-language reason.

5

The AI can't go rogue.

Code validates every plan; it can't quietly do something you didn't ask for.

6

We track behavior, never psychological labels.

We look at what you did, not who you "are."

7

We're honest about what still needs a human.

Essays and interviews get real human review. We don't pretend otherwise.
