This is a ready-to-build brief. Each text block is written twice: **EN** (English) and **中** (Chinese). ## 1. The one thing to get right (positioning spine)

We are **not** selling cheap AI advice, and **not** an "admissions chatbot." We're selling the thing families actually pay agencies for — **structure, pressure, and visible progress they can trust** — delivered continuously and affordably, with humans kept for what truly needs judgment.

> **Core line:** The operating system a great consultant *runs* — strategy, weekly tasks, deadlines, accountability, and progress everyone can see — without the $8,999 and without the guesswork.

*(Price anchor throughout = the average Irvine admissions consulting package: **$8,999 per application cycle**. Use this local number, not a national average — it lands harder with our actual families.)*

**Hard rule for all copy:** never say "AI replaces your counselor." We say *AI runs the repetitive, deadline-driven work; humans handle the high-trust moments (essays, interviews).* Overclaiming destroys 信任 with this audience. Honesty **is** the sales move.

**The through-line on every page: trust by design.** Nothing happens without a tap. Parents see progress, not private drafts. The student stays in control. This beats both the expensive opaque agency *and* the "just nag your kid yourself" alternative.

## 2A. Brand architecture (names)

**Master brand / company + product: Tandem 同舟** — *two in the same boat.* Parent and student, carried together; the supervision-without-surveillance promise lives in the name. This is the name on the door, the nav, the logo.

The three tiers are sub-brands that form one escalating journey — **拾阶 → 致远 → 北辰** (step by step → go the distance → guided by the star). Always pair each poetic name with a **plain-language label** so checkout is never confusing:

| Tier | Brand | Plain label | Price | Story |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | **Cadence 拾阶** | the planning core | $79/mo | step-by-step planning, the daily rhythm |
| Season | **Stride 致远** | the full application season | $699/yr | carries you the whole way to submit |
| Premium | **Lodestar 北辰** | with a real advisor | $2,999 | real human advisors as your guiding star |

Design note: when tiers appear together (pricing page, plan picker), show them as a **rising path** — the journey metaphor is the visual. The poetic name large, the plain label directly beneath it.

## 3. Design direction (tone + visual)

- **Mood:** calm, premium, trustworthy — *not* loud ed-tech. The vibe is "a system serious families rely on," with warmth.
- **Palette suggestion (swap for brand if it exists):** deep ink/navy as the dominant tone, one warm accent (amber or terracotta) for CTAs and highlights, generous white space. **Avoid generic ed-tech sky-blue and any "AI gradient" cliché.**
- **Type:** a serif for headlines (credibility/trust) paired with a clean sans for body.
- **Recurring motif:** the **approval checkpoint** — a small "you're in control here" marker wherever the product acts. Repeat it across pages; it visually carries the trust story.
- **Language:** **English-first by default**, but the page should quietly signal *we're a Chinese family too* — without being heavy-handed. Suggested subtle cues: the 陪跑 concept surfaced once as a named idea ("陪跑 — running alongside you"), a one-line bilingual welcome near the hero, and the founder section leaning into immigrant-family roots. The goal is a Chinese parent feels *"these are our people"* while an English-first student still feels it's built for them.
- **Toggle:** make it **obvious and easy to find** — a clearly labeled pill/segmented control in the top-right nav, **`EN | 中文`**, not a tiny globe icon. Default state = **EN**. It should be visible on every page and swap all copy instantly.
- **Hero visuals to mock up:** parent page → a *summary-only* progress dashboard (status + milestones, deliberately no essay text). Student page → a weekly calendar with a few small tasks + an "Approve plan" button. Tech page → the flow diagram in §7.

### 5.2 The relationship angle (emotional core — keep this prominent)
**EN** — Header: **Let the system do the nagging. You go back to being a parent.**
Every reminder, every deadline check, every "did you finish?" is handled by the copilot — not by you at the dinner table. The pressure comes from the system, so the relationship stays yours.

**中** — Header：**催进度的事交给系统，你只管当好爸妈。**

### 5.3 What you see — and what you don't
**EN** — Header: **What you see — and what you don't.**
- **You see:** on-track / behind status · milestone progress (school list, essay drafts, deadlines) · one concrete way to help this week.
- **You don't see:** their essays, their private notes, their every click.
- Why: we respect your child's space — because surveillance backfires, and trust is what keeps them moving.

**中** — Header：**哪些你看得到，哪些看不到。**

### 5.8 Parent FAQ
**EN**
- *Is this just a chatbot?* No — it's a system that plans, schedules, and tracks real progress. The chat is the smallest part.
- *Is it spying on my kid?* No. You see summary progress only. Essays and private notes are never shared.
- *Will it replace our school counselor or consultant?* No. It runs the repetitive accountability work; humans stay for judgment.

- *会取代升学顾问吗？* 不会。重复性的盯进度交给它，真正需要判断的环节，还是真人来做。
- *怎么收费？* **拾阶（$79/月）随时能退订**——想停就停，已经扣掉的那个月不退，但那个月你照常能用。**致远（$699/年）** 和 **北辰（$2,999）** 是一次性付清、不退款的——所以我们把入门价压得很低，建议你先用拾阶试试水。

## 7. Technical / Trust Page

### 7.1 Hero
**EN** — Headline: **How it works — and why you can trust it.**
Sub: **The AI suggests. Our system decides and protects you.**

**中** — Headline：**它是怎么运作的，你又为什么能放心交给它。**

### 7.2 The flow (diagram + caption)
Render as a horizontal flow with a highlighted **YOU APPROVE** checkpoint:

```
Your goal → AI proposes a plan → our code checks every plan → ★ YOU APPROVE ★ → it goes on your calendar → it adapts to how you actually do
```
**EN caption:** The AI never acts on its own. Every plan is checked by code before you ever see it, and nothing reaches your calendar until you approve it.
**中 caption：** AI 绝不自作主张。每一个计划在送到你面前之前，都会先被代码检查一遍；你没点头，任何东西都进不了你的日历。

### 7.3 Seven promises (each: promise + why it matters)
**EN**
1. **Nothing touches your calendar without your approval.** — You're always the last word.
2. **No double-bookings or messes — ever, even if something crashes.** — Your real calendar stays clean.
3. **Your private writing stays private; parents see only what you permit.** — Trust between you and your family, protected by design.
4. **The system always tells you why.** — Every change comes with a plain-language reason.
5. **The AI can't go rogue.** — Code validates every plan; it can't quietly do something you didn't ask for.
6. **We track behavior, never psychological labels.** — We look at what you did, not who you "are."
7. **We're honest about what still needs a human.** — Essays and interviews get real human review. We don't pretend otherwise.

**中**
1. **你不点头，它绝不动你的日历。** ——最后拍板的，永远是你。
2. **绝不重复、绝不撞车——就算程序中途崩了也不会。** ——你的日历不会被搞得乱七八糟。
3. **你的私人内容，始终只属于你；家长只看得到你允许的那部分。** ——你和家人之间的信任，系统从底层帮你守住。
4. **系统每次都会告诉你"为什么这么做"。** ——每一处改动，都配一句听得懂的解释。
5. **AI 不会脱缰。** ——每个计划都先过一遍代码检查，它没法偷偷做你没让它做的事。
6. **我们只看行为，绝不给人贴心理标签。** ——我们关心你做了什么，而不是急着给你"定性"。
7. **哪些地方还得靠真人，我们直说。** ——文书和面试都有真人把关，绝不含糊。
